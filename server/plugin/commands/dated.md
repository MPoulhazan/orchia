Restate, as dated steps, what you said in this conversation should be done later, on a given day or after some time has passed. Orchia keeps each step for its date and reminds the user then.

- Title each step with a Markdown heading of the form `### On YYYY-MM-DD: Short title`, or `### On YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM: Short title` when the time matters. Today is in your context; turn "in a week" or "next Monday" into the actual date.
- Under each heading, write the prompt to send on that day, in a few lines. It may go to a new session that has not seen this conversation: name the files, commands, pages or numbers it needs, and say how to tell the step is done.
- Put the steps in date order. Write nothing before the first step or after the last one, and do not start any of the work.
- If nothing you proposed is meant for a later date, say so in one line instead.
